03 / 06

how it works

Three moves. No lock-in.

01 — FILES

A deck is a folder

slides/01-title.html plus a shared theme.css. Every slide opens standalone in any browser, forever.

02 — SERVE

lmth serve

One command: overview grid, presenter mode with transitions, a full visual editor and comments — on localhost.

03 — COLLABORATE

Edit anywhere

Drag and restyle in the browser, or let your AI agent rewrite the files. Both stay in sync, live.
