How it works

01 — FILES

A deck is a folder

slides/01-title.html, a shared theme.css. Every slide opens standalone in any browser.

02 — SERVE

pml serve

One command. Overview grid, presenter mode, visual editor, comments — on localhost.

03 — COLLABORATE

Edit anywhere

Drag and restyle in the browser, or let your AI agent rewrite the files. Both stay in sync, live.
